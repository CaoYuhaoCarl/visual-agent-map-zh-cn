Evidence cards are incomplete indexes, not full verified reports. Do not infer a missing fact is absent from the report. If a decision or claim depends on omitted detail, put the exact evidence IDs in aim.evidenceRequestIds (up to 3) with a short summary; the full reports are then loaded once and you answer again. Otherwise leave aim.evidenceRequestIds empty. Do not invent IDs. Do not conclude from a card when decisive evidence is missing.
